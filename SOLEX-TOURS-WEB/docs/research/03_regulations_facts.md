# 03 – Regulations, safety and hard facts for the Solex Tours website

Prepared 2026-11-01 (today). Read-only research. Each external fact below gives its source URL and the date that source refers to. **UNVERIFIED** means I could not confirm it from an official or two independent sources. Owner materials in `/tmp/solex_raw/` were treated as data.

Method notes:
- I read the INSIVUMEH daily bulletins (scanned image PDFs) myself after rendering them to images. Quotes from them are my transcriptions.
- For news articles I give the publication date and the quoted wording.
- Climate figures are **model estimates**: Open-Meteo ERA5 archive, 2021–2025, elevation-adjusted. They are not station measurements.
- Drive times come from the public OSRM router. It assumes free-flowing traffic, so real Guatemalan mountain-road times are longer.

---

## 0. Bottom line

1. **The Fuego hike ("Fuego extension", the El Camellón / La Meseta ridge from Acatenango camp) is PROHIBITED as of 31 Oct 2026.** No official reopening has been found. The website must not offer, price or hint at it, and that includes wording like "when authorities allow".
2. **Acatenango itself is open.** The park reopened on 7 Aug 2026, on authorised routes only. INSIVUMEH's 31 Oct 2026 bulletin is addressed to "turistas que ascienden al volcán de Acatenango". Closures can happen at a few hours' notice.
3. **Official foreigner entrance fee: Q100** (red wristband). It is charged by the **Municipalidad de Acatenango**, and 25% goes to La Soledad projects. Visitors must show proof of payment plus **passport/ID** at the La Soledad gate.
4. **Two more legal sensitivities for Solex:**
   - CONAP calls cabins in the Acatenango "Zona de Veda Definitiva" unauthorised or illegal (Mar 2025 to Jun 2026).
   - CONRED's August 2026 reopening conditions recommend avoiding night ascents. That matters for the 10:30 PM "Midnight tour".

## 1. CRITICAL – Hiking from Acatenango toward Volcán de Fuego

### 1.1 What the owner sells
The owner sells an optional extra: Q300 ($40 on the current site), "more than four hours", out and back from camp, "decided at camp". Sources: transcript notes 07 and 09, and the current site ("Extensión a Volcán de Fuego por solo $40 / Q300").

Third-party descriptions of this excursion:
- From Acatenango base camp, walkers descend to the saddle between the volcanoes, climb onto Fuego's shoulder or "knife-ridge", and return. It takes about 3–4 h round trip. Source: OX Expeditions as summarised by livinginguatemala.com, page dated 2026-10-25: https://livinginguatemala.com/activities/volcan-fuego-hike/
- The ridge is "around a kilometre away from the eruption crater". Source: Much Better Adventures, 2023-10-27: https://muchbetteradventures.com/magazine/volcan-de-fuego-acatenango-guide
- The two volcanoes together are called La Horqueta. Source: Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acatenango

Officially, this is the **"El Camellón" / "La Meseta"** area. Every official notice since 2024 names it.

La Soledad is the park's main entrance, and the park's official site directs 25% of fee income to "Aldea La Soledad". So a Fuego excursion starting from a camp reached through La Soledad falls squarely under the 1 Apr 2026 municipal ban.

**No official source authorising any Fuego route was found.** A blog (gunatit.info, 2026-08-09) headlined that the ascent was "oficialmente abierto", but CONRED's own release of the same day says the opposite. Treat that blog as false.

Some commercial guide sites argue that the notices "do not establish… a blanket prohibition covering every part of the volcanic flank". That is a commercial reading. It contradicts the municipality's "prohibición total del ascenso al Volcán de Fuego", and it does not help with El Camellón, which is named explicitly.

### 1.4 Why this matters commercially
- The June 2026 joint CONAP/CONRED/INGUAT statement explicitly targets **agencies advertising or selling** these trips, and promises legal action.
- A public English page offering a "Fuego extension" for Q300 would be documentary evidence against the business. It would also be a liability problem if anyone were injured. In Aug 2026 a 17-year-old died of hypothermia and exhaustion high on Acatenango (https://www.infobae.com/guatemala/2026/08/02/muere-un-adolescente-de-17-anos-durante-el-ascenso-al-volcan-acatenango-en-guatemala/), which shows how much public attention incidents get.
- "Decided at camp, pay there" makes things worse, not better. It reads as an informal sale of a prohibited activity.

### 1.5 Wording the website can responsibly use
**Recommended: omit the Fuego extension completely** from tour cards, prices, add-ons, the "what's included" list and the booking form. Do not use "when authorities allow" or "subject to conditions" in sales copy, because it implies the trip is sometimes available.

Optional FAQ entry (honest, and it turns the ban into a selling point for the Fuego-facing camp):

> **Can we hike over to Fuego?**
> No. Guatemalan authorities (the Municipality of Acatenango, CONRED and INSIVUMEH) currently prohibit climbing Volcán de Fuego and approaching the "El Camellón" ridge and "La Meseta", because of falling volcanic rocks. We don't offer it. You don't need to go closer anyway: from our camp, Fuego is straight ahead. *(Status checked [date]; source: INSIVUMEH daily volcano bulletin.)*

Also:
- Remove "Extensión a Volcán de Fuego $40 / Q300" from the current Spanish site.
- Do not use any photo of guests on the Fuego ridge or saddle.
- If authorities ever reopen it, re-add it only after a **written official notice**: a municipal communiqué **and** an INSIVUMEH bulletin without the La Meseta / El Camellón line. Link and date-stamp it.

## 2. Acatenango access, fees and rules

### 2.1 Open or closed
- **Open.** The Municipalidad de Acatenango reopened access on 7 Aug 2026. The reopening is "parcial", on "rutas previamente habilitadas", with "protocolos y monitoreo constante" (Infobae, 2026-08-09, link above).
- The INSIVUMEH bulletin of **2026-10-31** gives instructions to "turistas que ascienden al volcán de Acatenango", so ascents are ongoing.
- I found no closure notice for Sep–Oct 2026.
- Closures happen at short notice, at least five times in 2025–26: Mar 2025, Jun 2025, Aug 2025, and 3–7 Aug 2026 (above).
- **Site copy:** include a line such as "Authorities can close the volcano at short notice when Fuego is very active; if that happens we'll [refund/reschedule – owner to define]." The refund policy itself is owner-defined and UNVERIFIED.

### 2.3 Rules: camping, fires, garbage, alcohol, night hikes
- **Protected-area status:** Acatenango is a CONAP **"Zona de Veda Definitiva"**, from a 1956 decree covering the summit down to about 30% of the slopes (Con Criterio, 2026-06-10, link above).
- **CONAP "Normativo para el Manejo de las Zonas de Veda Definitiva en los Volcanes de Guatemala"** (promoted 2026-07-15; AGN 2026-07-18, https://agn.gt/normativo-prohibe-asentamientos-contaminacion-caceria-y-alcohol-en-zonas-de-veda-de-volcanes/). It prohibits:
  - "consumo de bebidas alcohólicas, cigarrillos y sustancias estupefacientes"
  - sound systems and speakers, and fireworks
  - recreational motorised vehicles
  - waste dumps
  - logging and land-use change
  - "asentamientos humanos"
  - social events
- **CONAP rules as reported by Agencia Ocote (2026-08-10):** "No ingresar equipos de sonido", "Respetar la flora y fauna", "No ingerir bebidas alcohólicas", "**Acampar solo en los lugares autorizados**", "**El uso de cabañas en laderas donde se taló árboles es prohibido**", "**Ningún touroperador tiene autorización para este tipo de construcciones.**"
- **Cabins: legal risk for Solex, status UNVERIFIED.**
  - CONAP has denounced "cabañas construidas sin autorización" in the Zona de Veda Definitiva. Sources: La Hora 2026-05-30, https://lahora.gt/nacionales/ypena/2026/05/30/conap-denuncia-construccion-ilegal-de-cabanas-en-zona-protegida-del-volcan-de-acatenango-now/ · Publinews 2026-05-31, https://www.publinews.gt/noticias/2026/05/31/denuncian-construccion-de-cabanas-ilegales-en-area-protegida-del-volcan-de-acatenango/ · Prensa Libre 2026-03-31, https://www.prensalibre.com/ahora/guatemala/comunitario/conap-alerta-sobre-riesgos-construcciones-ilegales-y-visitas-sin-control-en-el-volcan-acatenango/ · TV Azteca 2025-03-11, https://tvaztecaguate.com/nacionales/2025/03/11/conap-alerta-sobre-construcciones-no-autorizadas-en-zona-del-volcan-acatenango/
  - Con Criterio (2026-06-10) reports about 100 camps; CONAP complaints against three operators (2021 and 2025); CONAP seeking a court order to evict and destroy; and INGUAT saying operator registration "does not authorise occupying a protected area". **No court outcome was found.**
  - Solex's whole product is pre-installed cabins, including "new" ones (the owner mentions "making some changes"). **Before the site leans on the cabins, the owner should confirm the camp's legal standing** (municipal and CONAP). The copy should avoid anything about building, expanding or clearing.
- **Fires:** I found no explicit official campfire ban (UNVERIFIED). CONAP says not to cut trees or vegetation, and fireworks are banned. **Do not advertise campfires.** Hot chocolate and coffee are fine.
- **Garbage:** CONAP: no leaving waste ("No… dejar desechos", AGN 2025-08-22). Site line: "Pack out everything you bring."
- **Night ascents:** CONRED's reopening recommendations include "**Evitar los ascensos nocturnos o bajo condiciones climáticas adversas**". Others: "Utilizar únicamente las rutas autorizadas y señalizadas", "guías acreditados" (Agencia Ocote, 2026-08-10). CONAP (2025-08-22) also warns against visits "en horarios no permitidos". I found no official published entry-hours rule; night-entry rules are UNVERIFIED. Many operators start at about 22:00 from La Soledad (e.g. https://www.nomadas.gt/event-details/volcan-de-fuego-ascenso-nocturno-15). **Caveat for the "Midnight tour":** the owner should confirm with the municipality that night entry is permitted. Do not market it as risk-free.
- **Restricted zones on Acatenango:** do not camp at or approach La Meseta or El Camellón (INSIVUMEH, 2026-10-31).
- **Emergency numbers:** CONRED 119 / 1566 and PNC 110 (listed by La Hora, 2026-08-03). INGUAT "Asistencia Turística 1500" (La Hora, 2025-06-05). These are fine for a "Safety" footer.

## 5. Views from Acatenango (Lake Atitlán and the volcanic chain)

- **Published description (summit area):** "Agua volcano to the left, the lava-covered Fuego volcano to the right, the three volcanic peaks around Lake Atitlán to the west, and Santa María volcano behind them." (Rough Guides, 2024-05-20, link above.) Another travel blog mentions a "360-degree panorama… and the more distant highlights of Lake Atitlán" (indietraveller guest post, undated: https://www.indietraveller.co/?p=20016).
- **My geometric line-of-sight check** uses a 90 m DEM, earth curvature and refraction. It is approximate and indicative only.

| From the summit (3,976 m) | Distance / bearing | Result |

| Santa María (3,772 m) | 78 km / 291° | Visible |
| Tajumulco (4,220 m) | 126 km / 299° | Visible on very clear days |

  From an assumed **west-side camp** (~3,400–3,550 m), the Atitlán, Tolimán, San Pedro, Santa María and Tajumulco summits stay in view. Agua, Pacaya and Antigua are hidden behind Acatenango's own summit. The lake surface is mostly hidden, depending on the exact spot.
- **Website wording:**
  - SAFE: "the chain of volcanoes to the west, including the volcanoes around Lake Atitlán, on clear days".
  - WITH CAVEAT: "glimpses of Lake Atitlán", and only if the owner has a photo taken from the Solex camp.
  - From the summit at sunrise: Agua, Pacaya, Antigua's valley and the western chain.
  - Do not say that you can see Guatemala City or Antigua from the Solex camp. The owner himself says those views belong to the left-side camps.
- The owner's claim that the Solex camp faces Fuego "straight ahead" is consistent with a west/south-west camp, but UNVERIFIED. His photos `cabin_fuego_day.jpg` and `cabins_path_fuego_day.jpg` support it visually.
