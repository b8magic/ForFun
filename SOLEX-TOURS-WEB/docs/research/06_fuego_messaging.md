## 1. Operator-by-operator findings

The descriptive text says "One trail leads you towards the enigmatic Volcan de Fuego, where fiery displays captivate and remind you of nature's untamed power."

## 3. Patterns worth borrowing

1. **Treat it as the "second trail" from camp.** Lava Trails: "two enticing trails beckon you". The summit is included, and Fuego is the optional second trail. This framing makes the add-on feel natural and not like an upsell.
2. **Put the decision moment in the copy.** "you can make this decision at Basecamp" (Lava Trails). "No rush on deciding… when you book" (Wicho & Charlie's). This is the strongest conversion line in the category, yet every competitor buries it in an FAQ, T&C or body paragraph. Solex should make it the headline.
3. **Use 3-step micro-structure.** BlueSky's "Arrival at Base Camp → The Hike → The Reward" is easy to scan on mobile. For Solex: "Reach camp → Decide → Go closer (or stay and watch)".
4. **Make the itinerary line do double duty.** Every operator puts a one-line "16:00 Optional Fuego hike (+price)" in the timeline. Solex should do the same, so the extra is seen in context.
5. **Show the price in a separate "Optional extras" box** next to the main price (OX "Optional Extras", Lava Trails "EXCLUDED"). It keeps the Q750 headline price clean and honest.
6. **Use honest, slightly understated duration.** "approx. a 4 hour hike (2 hours going up and 2 hours going back)" is concrete and believable. Solex's "about 4 hours, sometimes more" sounds just as credible.
7. **Describe the sensory payoff without a guarantee.** The best line found is "you see those explosions far closer than from camp, and you feel them" (affiliate guide). Pair any sensory promise with "when Fuego is active" or "on a clear night".
8. **Make camp a good outcome too.** "For everyone else, camp is the better seat: you watch Fuego from a sleeping bag, with dinner and a fire." This takes the pressure off and fits Solex's front-row camp.
9. **Give practical gear lines with a reason.** "A headlamp: you walk back in the dark." "The secret is to layer-up!" "Bring an extra 500+ calorie snack." (Use these only once Alexander confirms timing and gear.)

## 4. Recommendation for Solex

### 4.1 Name and placement
- **Label:** "Optional Fuego Hike". This is the most common term and matches "Fuego hike" searches. Use "extra hike from camp toward Fuego" in body copy. Avoid "summit", "crater", "ridge" (not in Alexander's facts), "Double Whammy" and "OX-Treme" (OX's names).
- **Where:** (1) an add-on card directly under the overnight tour price, (2) a one-line itinerary entry after "arrive at camp", (3) the FAQ entry below, (4) optionally a small badge in the tour summary: "Optional Fuego hike: decide at camp, pay only if you go". Keep it out of the hero, which should sell the overnight tour and the front-row camp.

### 4.2 Add-on card copy (based only on Alexander's facts; 70–82 words each including headline)

**Option A: "Decide at camp" lead** (82 words)
> **Want to get closer to Fuego? Decide at camp.**
> From our camp, Alexander offers an optional extra hike toward Volcán de Fuego to bring you closer to it. Plan on about 4 hours, sometimes more, then you come back to the same camp to sleep. You choose once you are up there: if you feel fit and the weather is right, you go. It costs +Q300 per person, paid at camp. Nothing is prepaid, so if you skip it, you lose nothing.

**Option B: "Front-row camp" lead** (70 words)
> **Front row at camp. Closer if you want it.**
> Our camp sits on the right-hand side of Acatenango, facing Fuego straight on, so the show starts when you arrive. Still have energy left? Join the optional extra hike from camp toward Fuego to get closer: about 4 hours, sometimes more, and back to the same camp for the night. +Q300 per person, paid at camp, and only if you go.

*(Before publishing, check "right-hand side" with Alexander: right-hand side as seen from where? Consider "on the side of Acatenango that faces Fuego straight on" if the reference point isn't obvious to a visitor.)*

**Option C: "No prepayment, no pressure" lead** (76 words)
> **The Fuego extra hike: no prepayment, no pressure**
> Some guests want more than the view from camp. For them, Alexander offers an optional extra hike toward Volcán de Fuego. It takes about 4 hours, sometimes more, and you sleep back at the same camp afterwards. There is nothing to book or pay in advance. Decide at camp, based on how your legs feel and what the weather is doing, and pay the +Q300 per person there.

**Optional lines to add only once Alexander confirms them** (not part of his brief):
- Timing: "Leaves camp at about [time], back for dinner." Every competitor leaves between 15:00 and 17:00.
- Gear: "Bring your headlamp and an extra warm layer. You'll walk back in the dark." (only if the hike does return after dark)
- Who decides: "Your guide has the final word on the day." (only if true; Alexander's brief says the guest decides)
- Food: "Pack an extra snack." (Wicho & Charlie's and AKT both ask for 500+ calories)

### 4.3 FAQ: "Can I hike to Fuego?"
> Yes, as an optional extra from our Acatenango camp: a hike of about 4 hours (sometimes more) toward Fuego to get closer, then back to the same camp to sleep; you decide at camp, depending on your fitness and the weather, and pay +Q300 per person there, with nothing paid in advance.

### 4.4 Conversion angles: where Solex is stronger, and where it isn't

1. **Decide at camp: lead with it.** Lava Trails, Wicho & Charlie's and OX also let guests decide at camp, so this isn't unique. But all three hide it in an FAQ, T&C clause or "What's the difference?" box. Solex wins by putting it in the headline. Key phrase: "Decide at camp."
2. **Pay only if you go, with no prepayment.** BlueSky ("Guests who have booked the add-on"), AKT (sold online with a 48-hour cancellation rule) and the OX Double Whammy pre-booking all put money at stake before the climb. Wicho & Charlie's and AKT also charge in full once you start, "whether you complete it or not", and "regardless of the visibility". Solex never needs a refund policy for this extra because nothing is collected in advance. Say "pay only if you go" and "nothing to lose". Don't say "refund" at all.
   - **Gap to close with Alexander:** if a guest starts and turns back early, is the Q300 still due? Competitors spell this out. Solex copy shouldn't imply "pay only if you finish" unless he confirms it.
3. **Front-row camp: be specific, not superlative.** "Facing Fuego" is common: BlueSky ("facing volcano Fuego"), CA Travelers ("just in front of the Fuego Volcano"), AKT ("directly in front of our camp", "one of the closest points"), Tropicana ("straight across at eye level"). Solex should win on specifics, such as its actual position, a real photo taken from the camp, and the line that skipping the extra hike still leaves you with the show. It shouldn't claim "closest".
4. **Camp is good either way.** Because the camp faces Fuego directly, Solex can say that skipping the extra hike isn't missing out. Competitors with weaker camp views can't say this as convincingly, and it supports the no-pressure promise.
5. **A trailhead-local voice.** "Alexander offers…" from La Soledad itself contrasts with the corporate "Rules for the Hike" and T&C tone at AKT, Wicho & Charlie's and Lava Trails.
6. **Honest duration.** "About 4 hours, sometimes more" beats contradictory or minimizing figures: Wicho & Charlie's gives 3 h, 4–5 h and 5 h on different pages, and Extreme Life says "at only 1.5 hours". Honesty here builds trust for the whole page.
7. **Price is not the angle.** Q300 is at the top of the market range (Lava Trails and Soy Q250, Wicho & Charlie's and AKT Q300, OX US$40, BlueSky US$43, Extreme Life US$35). Show it plainly in quetzales next to the Q750 base price, and don't compare prices.

### 4.5 Phrases to AVOID

**Overpromising lava or proximity**
- "summit of Fuego", "to the crater", "crater rim" (Extreme Life, Wicho & Charlie's, AKT main tour). Solex's hike goes *toward* Fuego.
- Any distance to the crater: "as close as 150m to the exploding crater" (Wicho & Charlie's), "400 meters from the crater" (Extreme Life), "200 m from the eruptions" (affiliate).
- "get as close as possible" (Lava Trails FAQ) or "as close as is safely possible" (OX). These are vague but imply pushing limits.
- "lava up close", "lava show", "see the lava", "lava every 15 minutes", "constantly erupting", "always erupting".
- "sunset at the summit" or "sunset on Fuego", since Alexander hasn't confirmed timing.
- Unsourced success rates such as "90% of groups see eruptions" (CA Travelers), unless Solex has its own data.

**Guarantees and absolute safety**
- "guaranteed", "we guarantee safe excursions" (Soy Tours), "100% safe", "at a safe distance" (Tropicana, Extreme Life), "we don't lose anyone".
- "closest camp to Fuego" or "the closest point" (AKT's claim; unverifiable).
- "for everyone" or "easy". Competitors use "IS NOT FOR EVERYONE!" or "top physical condition", but the Solex facts support "if you feel fit", which is enough.

**Anything legal or authority-related (client rule)**
- "permitted", "authorised/authorized", "approved", "official route", "legal", "licensed", "registered and authorised by" (Extreme Life), "certified".
- Agency names: CONRED, INSIVUMEH, CONAP, INGUAT. Also "closure", "ban", "government advisory".
- "liability form" or "waiver" in marketing copy (Wicho & Charlie's and AKT put these in their rules lists).

**Tone and pricing traps**
- "Once you start there is no going back", "Please don't insist" (Wicho & Charlie's, AKT). These are policy language, not sales copy.
- "refund", "non-refundable", "free cancellation" for the extra. Nothing is prepaid, so these words only confuse.
- "Double Whammy", "OX-Treme", "angry beast", "knife-ridge". These are OX's voice and names.

## 5. Context note for the client (not website copy)

The client chose not to make legal or authority statements on the site, and none of the copy above does. For awareness only, here is what competitors and third parties say publicly, which customers may read before booking:
- Wicho & Charlie's refund policy covers "local or national authorities issue advisories or evacuation orders". Extreme Life's policy covers "the park closes" and "state of emergency". https://wichoandcharlies.com/fuego-hike · https://extremelifetours.com/our-policies
